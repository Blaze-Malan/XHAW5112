_FREQUENTLY ASKED

EVERYTHING YOU
NEED TO KNOW.

  • DO I NEED TO BOOK IN ADVANCE?+

    Walk-ins are welcome when stations are available, but booking ahead is the best way to secure your preferred time. The online booking calculator is a demo and does not make a reservation. Please arrive 10 minutes before your session.

  • CAN I BRING MY OWN PERIPHERALS?+

    Yes. You can bring a wired USB keyboard, mouse, or headset. Our team can help connect your gear; please don't install software or change settings on the arena PCs.

  • IS THERE AN AGE RESTRICTION?+

    Players of all ages are welcome. Guests under 16 must be accompanied by an adult, and age ratings still apply to individual games and events.

  • DO I NEED MY OWN GAME ACCOUNTS?+

    Our game library is installed on the arena PCs. Sign in with your own account to access your profile and progress, then remember to sign out at the end of your session. Never share your password with anyone.

  • CAN I ORDER FOOD WHILE PLAYING?+

    Snacks and non-alcoholic drinks are available from the counter. Please enjoy food in the lounge area and keep it away from the gaming PCs to protect the equipment.
