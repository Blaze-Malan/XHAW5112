ARENA POLICIES AT A GLANCE.

Quick reference to the arena's visitor policies
TOPIC ARENA POLICY QUICK TIP
Bookings Walk-ins are welcome when stations are available; confirmed bookings take priority. Request a booking ahead and arrive 10 minutes early.
Age and access Guests under 16 must be accompanied by an adult. Game and event age ratings apply. Check the rating before choosing a game.
Equipment Wired USB keyboards, mice, and headsets are welcome. Use the arena PCs as set up; don't install software or change settings.
Game accounts Players sign in with their own account to access personal profiles and progress. Sign out after playing and never share your password.
Food and drink Snacks and non-alcoholic drinks are available from the counter. Enjoy food in the lounge and keep it away from gaming PCs.
